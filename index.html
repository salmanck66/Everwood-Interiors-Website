Our Story

From Vision
to Reality

Everwood Interiors was born from a craftsman's dream — the belief that a well-designed space has the power to transform the people who live in it. Every nail, every plank, every finish reflects that belief.

Based in Areekode, Malappuram, we've grown from a passionate two-person team into one of Malabar's most trusted interior studios — blending traditional Kerala craftsmanship with modern, forward-thinking aesthetics.

We don't just design interiors. We listen to your life, understand your aspirations, and build spaces that feel like they were always meant to be yours — at a price that makes sense.
